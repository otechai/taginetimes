## This is satire

Tagine Times is a work of **satire and parody**. Every article is a humorous, exaggerated commentary on public institutions, policies, and social patterns in Morocco — not a factual news report.

Some concrete rules we hold ourselves to, so this disclaimer means something instead of just covering us:

- **We do not name private individuals** in connection with wrongdoing, and we do not attribute invented actions, statements, or scandals to any named person, public or private.
- **We do not fabricate quotes, statistics, or events.** Where a figure, law, or institutional finding appears in an article, it is drawn from a real, checkable public source — HCP, Bank Al-Maghrib, the Cour des Comptes, official gazettes (*Bulletin Officiel*), or comparable public records.
- **Exaggeration, hyperbole, and invented scenarios are the mechanism of the joke**, layered on top of that real, sourced foundation — not a substitute for it. If an article describes a scene, a form, a receipt, or a dialogue, treat it as illustrative fiction built to make a documented pattern legible, not as a transcript of a specific real event.
- **Institutions and archetypes are our subject, not private reputations.** When we write about "the Ministry" or "a regional hospital," we are describing a pattern drawn from public data, not accusing a specific office-holder of a specific undisclosed act.

If you are a public official, an institution, or any person who believes a specific article about you personally is being mistaken for a factual claim, contact us and we will address it.

Nothing on this site should be republished, screenshotted, or cited as factual reporting.
