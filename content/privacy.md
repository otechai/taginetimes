## Short version

Tagine Times does not run analytics, does not use tracking cookies, does not have a comments system, and does not ask you to create an account. There is very little to say here, on purpose.

## What actually happens when you visit

This site is a static set of HTML pages with no server-side application and no database. When you load a page:

- Your browser requests files directly from our hosting/CDN provider. That provider's infrastructure necessarily logs standard web-server data (IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamp) for operational and security purposes, the same way any web host does. We do not receive or store this data ourselves, and we do not access it for tracking purposes.
- No first-party cookies are set by this site.
- No third-party analytics, advertising, or social-media tracking pixels are embedded in any page.
- The on-page search box runs entirely in your browser against a local index generated at build time. Your search terms are never sent anywhere.

## If this changes

If we ever add privacy-respecting, cookie-less analytics (to see which articles are read, nothing more), this page will be updated first, and any such tool will not sell or share data with third parties.

## Third-party links

Articles link out to public sources — HCP reports, official gazettes, news coverage. Once you click through, that site's own privacy policy applies, not this one.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the [About](/about/) page.
